# P3 — Teknik İngilizce (sürekli)

Hedef: Bir teknik problemi 3–5 dakika akıcı anlatmak ve takip sorularına cevap vermek. Okuma güçlü; çalışılacak taraf konuşma ve yazma.

## Günlük rutin (15–20 dk, her çalışma günü)

1. **Sesli okuma (5 dk):** O modülün okuma listesindeki makalelerden birinin abstract'ını yüksek sesle oku.
2. **Bugünü anlat (5 dk):** O gün yaptığın egzersizleri kayda alarak anlat. Kalıpları kullan: *The goal was… / I started by… / I changed only… / The result was… / The main problem was… / Next, I would…*
3. **Sözlük (5 dk):** Günde 5 terim. `glossary.md` dosyasına terim, kendi cümlen ve Türkçe karşılığı.

## Modül sonu

- RND raporunu İngilizce yaz (zaten şablon İngilizce).
- RND'yi **3 dakikada** kayda alarak anlat. Akış: Context → Question → What I did → Result → Limitation → Next.
- Kaydı bir hafta sonra dinle; en çok takıldığın 3 ifadeyi sözlüğe ekle.

## Faz sonu: mülakat provası

Her soruya 2 dakikalık cevap kaydet.

**Faz A–B**
1. Explain the bias–variance trade-off with an example from your work.
2. What is data leakage? Give a case you caught.
3. Walk me through backpropagation for a two-layer network.
4. Why does Adam usually converge faster than SGD? When might SGD be better?
5. How do you decide whether a 1% improvement is real?

**Faz C**
1. How is mAP computed? What does mAP@0.5:0.95 add?
2. Your detector misses small windows. What do you try, in what order?
3. Semantic vs instance segmentation — when do you need which?
4. How do you write a labeling guideline and measure label quality?
5. Describe your Kaggle validation strategy and how it matched the leaderboard.

**Faz D**
1. Explain a homography and when it is valid.
2. What does MASt3R output, and how did you evaluate its depth?
3. Explain classifier-free guidance.
4. What does ControlNet's zero convolution do?
5. How did you measure cross-view consistency in MassToRender, and what are the metric's limits?
6. How would you deploy a model with p95 latency under 100 ms on a 6 GB GPU?

## Kalıplar

- "The main goal was to…" · "I started with a baseline that…" · "I changed only one thing:…"
- "The first approach did not work because…" · "The improvement is within the noise / outside the confidence interval."
- "The main limitation is…" · "If I continued, I would…"
